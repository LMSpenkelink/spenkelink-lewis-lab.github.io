Congratulations to Nate Ruddock and Will Heaton who culminated their Honours year with a final seminar. 

Both presentations highlighted their research projects and the progress they have made throughout their time in our lab.

Nate presented his work on affordable cell-free expression systems, discussing his novel, simple, and affordable in-house cell-free expression kit, which does away with traditional _in vivo_ methods.

Will presented his work on nanobody-affinity grids, focusing on how these grids can enrich tagged particles for cryogenic-electron microscopy.

Congratulations to both on their presentations and their amazing contributions to the lab!
