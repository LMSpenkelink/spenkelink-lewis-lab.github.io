title: Nicholas Kusi-Appauh is a finalist at the Thompson Prize Symposium
date: 2025-11-28
---

Congratulations to Nicholas Kusi for presenting at the Sydney Protein Group Thompson Prize Symposium.

<!--more-->

Nicholas was selected as a finalist for the Thompson Prize. Organised by the Sydney Protein Group, the prize is awarded for the best presentation by a young local scientist in the field of protein structure and function. 

Nick presented his work on "Single-molecule fluorescence visualisation of DNA polymerase dynamics at G-quadruplexes".
