---
title: Jordan Nicholls receives best talk award at ECPM 2026
date: 2026-07-24
---

Our entire lab travelled to Coffs Harbour for the 2026 East Coast Protein Meeting, where Jordan Nicholls was selected for an ASBMB–KSBMB award following his presentation.

<!--more-->

Members of our lab attended the 2026 East Coast Protein Meeting in Coffs Harbour, joining researchers from across the east coast to present and discuss their latest research.

A huge congratulations to Jordan Nicholls, whose presentation was recognised with an ASBMB–KSBMB award. The award provides Jordan with the opportunity to attend the 3rd Joint Australian & Korean Societies of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology Symposium later this year.
