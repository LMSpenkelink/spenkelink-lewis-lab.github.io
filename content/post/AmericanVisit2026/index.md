We were delighted to welcome our collaborators from the University of Southern California and the University of Wisconsin–Madison to Molecular Horizons.

Members of our lab worked closely with researchers from both universities, bringing together our respective technical expertise and approaches to advance our collaborative research.

The visit provided a valuable opportunity to work together in person, exchange ideas, and strengthen our ongoing international collaborations.
