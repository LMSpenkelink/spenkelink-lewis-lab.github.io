title: "Jordan Cater wins Best ECR Speaker at ASB 2025"
date: 2025-11-26
summary: "Congratulations to Dr Jordan Cater, who received the Best ECR Speaker award at ASB 2025 for his work developing generalisable Quenchbody biosensors."
---

Congratulations to Dr Jordan Cater, who was awarded Best ECR Speaker at the Australian Society for Biophysics Annual meeting 2025.

Jordan presented his research on the development of generalisable Quenchbody biosensors based on a synthetic nanobody library, highlighting a new approach for developing versatile biosensing tools.

The award recognised Jordan's outstanding presentation and research, and was a fantastic achievement at this year's conference.
