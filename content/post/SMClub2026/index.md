Callum and Via presented their work on single-molecule directed evolution at the Single-molecule Discussion Club.

<!--more-->

The Single-molecule Discussion Club brings together single-molecule research groups from across New South Wales, giving researchers the opportunity to share their latest work, exchange ideas, and discuss new developments in the field.

Callum and Via represented our lab at the June meeting, presenting their research on the development of single-molecule directed evolution methods.
