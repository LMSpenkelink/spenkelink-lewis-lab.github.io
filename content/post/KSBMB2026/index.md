Dr Jordan Nicholls presented at the **ASBMB/KSBMB Joint Symposium, held at the University of Sydney on 28 September 2026.

His talk, "Capturing Human DNA Replication in Action: A multi-platform toolkit for multi-subunit complexes", showcased his work developing and combining complementary approaches to study the complex molecular machinery responsible for human DNA replication.

The symposium brought together researchers from the Australian and Korean Societies for Biochemistry and Molecular Biology to share emerging technologies and discoveries across the field.
