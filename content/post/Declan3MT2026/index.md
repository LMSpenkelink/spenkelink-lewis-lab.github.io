---
title: "Declan wins 3MT Faculty Heat"
date: 2026-06-10
summary: "Congratulations to Declan Fisher, who won the SMAH heat for UOW's Three Minute Thesis competition."
---

Congratulations to PhD student Declan Fisher, who won the Faculty of Science, Medicine and Health heat of the University of Wollongong "Three Minute Thesis" (3MT) competition.

Declan presented his PhD research on how human cells respond to and repair DNA damage during replication, communicating his work to a general audience in just three minutes.

His win will see him progress to the University of Wollongong 3MT Final in Jult, representing the Faculty alongside finalists from across the University.
